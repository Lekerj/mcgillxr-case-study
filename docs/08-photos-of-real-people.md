# 8. Photos of real people

[← Previous](07-moving-the-domain.md) · [Back](../README.md)

## The problem

A public club website is full of students' faces, and some of those photos raised questions
that had nothing to do with code.

## Hidden data in photos

Camera data was still embedded in the published images. I stripped it from every one and
kept the originals privately.

## Giving credit

I tracked down photographers wherever I could and credited them on the site. When I couldn't
be sure who took a photo, I left it uncredited rather than guess.

## Consent

One event's only photos showed children, and there was no record that their parents had
agreed. I took the event off the site.

## Quebec privacy law

The privacy page names a contact by role, so it stays valid when the team changes. It also
explains how anyone can opt out of event photos.
