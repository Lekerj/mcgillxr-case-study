# 8. Photos of real people

[← Previous](07-moving-the-domain.md) · [Back](../README.md)

**Problem.** A public club site is full of students' faces.

**Calls I made.**

- **Metadata.** I stripped camera data from every published image and kept the originals
  privately.
- **Credit.** I traced photographers where I could and credited them. Where I couldn't, I
  left the photo uncredited rather than guess.
- **Consent.** One event's only photos showed children, with no record of consent. I took
  the event down.
- **Law 25.** The privacy page names a contact by role, so it survives turnover, and explains
  how to opt out of photos.
