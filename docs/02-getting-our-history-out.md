# 2. Our history only existed behind a login

[← Previous](01-nobody-could-see-the-site.md) · [Back](../README.md) · [Next →](03-the-old-site-was-wrong.md)

**Problem.** Four years of events and photos were only on the private site.

**What failed.** Plain requests got redirected to the login page. The Wayback Machine had
the text but none of the images.

![Archived page with every image missing](../screenshots/old-past-events-2024-full.jpg)

**What worked.** I scraped it with a real browser signed in as the club. Across the club site
and the CareXR hackathon site, that recovered about 280 photos. I filled the gaps from Luma,
Devpost and photos of event banners and shirts from social media platforms like Instagram.

**Cleanup.** I deleted the saved login afterwards, and kept the scraper and raw data out of
the website repo.
