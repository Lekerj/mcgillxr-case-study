# 2. Our history was behind a login

[← Previous](01-nobody-could-see-the-site.md) · [Home](../README.md) · [Next →](03-the-old-site-was-wrong.md)

## The problem

Most of four years of events, photos and sponsors lived on the private Google Site.

## What didn't work

Regular requests got redirected straight to the login page. The Wayback Machine had saved
the text from when the site was public, but none of the images.

![Archived page with every image missing](../screenshots/old-past-events-2024-full.jpg)

## What did

I scraped the site with a real browser, signed in with an account that had access. Between the club site and the
CareXR hackathon site, I recovered about 280 photos. For anything still missing, I went to
Luma, Devpost, and photos of event banners and shirts posted on Instagram and other social
media.

## Cleaning up

Once the scrape was done, I deleted the saved login. I also kept the scraper and the raw data
out of the website repo, so it only contains the website.
